Mérida — the nearer culture alternative

Mérida suits cruise passengers who want plazas, colonial streets, markets and food without the long highway profile of Chichén Itzá. It is commonly discussed as roughly 30–45 minutes inland from Progreso, but treat that as a typical range and confirm on the day.
