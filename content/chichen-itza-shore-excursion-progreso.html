Chichén Itzá from Progreso — plan the long day carefully

Chichén Itzá is the iconic inland ruins day many passengers associate with Progreso. El Castillo and the wider archaeological zone are the draw. The trade-off is coach time: treat the journey as a substantial inland commitment and verify timings against your all-aboard clock.

Common planning discussion places drive time around two hours each way, but that is a planning heuristic, not a promise. Traffic, stops and operator routing change outcomes. Combo days that add a cenote (often Ik Kil) need even clearer timing math.

  • ✓ Best when your port window is genuinely long.
  • ✓ Bring sun protection — shade is limited on the main plaza.
  • ✓ Compare with Uxmal if you prefer a different ruins character.
  • ✓ No payment or live availability on this website.
