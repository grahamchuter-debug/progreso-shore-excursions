Methodology

This site is editorial. We do not invent star ratings, review counts, bestsellers or fabricated availability.

Drive times and crowd descriptions are framed as planning ranges or commonly discussed context, not guarantees. Where geography is easy to conflate (for example nearer pink-lagoon wetlands versus distant Las Coloradas), we narrow claims deliberately.

Ship schedule pages and direct payment are deferred in this phase.

See also about. Image provenance is recorded in the repository file images/ATTRIBUTION.md.
