Uxmal from Progreso — culture first, no booking theatre

Uxmal is a strong candidate for cruise passengers who want Mayan architecture with a different character from Chichén Itzá. The Pyramid of the Magician and Puuc stonework reward slower looking. What to weigh

Planning factors — not a sales ranking.

Architecture

Puuc façades and site layout are the main reason many visitors prefer Uxmal as a cultural day.

Port-day fit

Needs enough hours ashore for coach time, site time and a return buffer. Confirm your ship’s window.

Crowding

Often described as quieter than Chichén, but that is not a universal rule. Visit conditions change.
